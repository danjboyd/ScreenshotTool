ScreenshotTool 0.3.0 adds Share on every platform. Send the annotated image, or just the selection, to mail, the clipboard, another app, a cloud drive or one of your custom actions. You can share from wherever you are working.

This release also includes everything from 0.2.1-rc1, which never became a final release: a right-click menu on the image and a Crop · Copy · Redact bar next to a selection.

## Upgrading

Use Check for Updates, or wait for the automatic check. From 0.1.1 or later, the update installs from inside the app on macOS, Windows and Linux.

## Share

- **Where to find it:** the toolbar's Share button, File ▸ Share…, Share… in the image's right-click menu, and Share on the selection's bar (#260, #263, #265, #267)
- **What it sends:** the selection when there is one, or else the whole annotated image, the same as Copy. Custom actions follow the same rule now; before, they always sent the whole image (#260)
- **Linux and Windows** get a share sheet that previews the image being shared. It offers Email, Copy (as both the picture and the file), Save a Copy, Show in Folder and Open With…. If `rclone` is on your PATH, the sheet also lists one entry for each rclone remote that can make links. That entry uploads the image with progress and a Cancel button, then copies the link (#263, #264, #265)
- **Custom actions** appear in every Share menu, after the system's services. An action with choices appears once per choice (#272)
- **Word of how a share went:** after a share ends, the app says "Shared with …", "Saved a copy", or why the share failed. A cancel shows no message. Email, Show in Folder and Open With show nothing when they hand the image to another app, because that app's window opening is the sign it worked; a failure is still reported (#272, #274)
- **Mail subject (macOS):** the image's name, or "Screenshot" (#272)
- **macOS:** Share… from the right-click menu opens at the click (#271)
- **Linux:** drag the Copy button into another app, such as a browser, a chat or a file manager, to drop in the image as a PNG, the same as on macOS. A click still copies (#262)

## Right-click menu and selection bar

- A right-click on the image shows a menu for what you clicked. In a selection it offers Crop to Selection, Copy Selection, Redact Selection and Deselect. On an annotation it offers Edit Text, Duplicate, Bring to Front, Send to Back and Delete. Anywhere else on the image it offers Copy Image, Paste as New Image, zoom, Save As… and your custom actions (#251)
- When you finish drawing a selection, a small bar with Crop, Copy and Redact appears next to it, and now Share as well. To turn the bar off, go to Preferences ▸ Appearance (#254)
- The Edit menu adds Redact Selection, Duplicate, Bring to Front and Send to Back (#253)
- On a narrow window, Save and Copy keep their names in the toolbar's » menu. Before, they showed there as blank rows (#266)

## Windows

- Share is new, with the share sheet described above (#265)
- Save no longer fails with "An unknown error occurred" when another program has the file open for a moment. Defender or a thumbnail scan can do that. Save now retries briefly, and if the file stays locked, the message names the file (#269)
- Uses WinUITheme 0.1.0-alpha9. The toolbar colour button draws as a WinUI colour drop-down. The window no longer comes back 32 px shorter at every launch (#259)

## Linux

- Resizing the window is much faster with a large image open (#257)
- Uses the Adwaita theme 0.1.0-alpha14. In a narrow window, the title is truncated before the » button instead of being drawn under it (#256)

## macOS

- Copy, Save and Share keep the image's exact pixels and colour space. Before, an opened PNG could have its colours shifted to the display's colour space, so pure red became 255,38,0 (#270, fixes #229)

## Known issues

- **Windows:** Email reports that the share is done as soon as you click it, not when Outlook's message window returns. A failure later in Outlook may go unreported. A fix will come in a later release.
- **Linux on Wayland:** the share sheet's Copy writes only the X11 clipboard, so apps that read only the Wayland clipboard won't see it. The message says "Copied image to X11 clipboard". The toolbar's Copy button writes to both clipboards.
- **Linux and Windows:** in the share sheet, a custom action with choices shows a tile for each choice. The tiles have no icon and their names are cut short, so the tiles look alike. Hover over one to see its full name. The right choice runs (#279).
- **macOS:** picking a custom action from a Share menu says "Shared with …" as soon as the action starts. If the action then fails, its error follows a few seconds later (#280).

The Windows MSI and the macOS app are still unsigned. See "Opening an unsigned app.txt" in the DMG. In Windows SmartScreen, click "More info → Run anyway".
