ScreenshotTool 0.3.5 is a patch release. It fixes automatic update checks that could stop on Windows and Linux, and a repeated update offer, and Redact Emails and Phone Numbers finds far more items.

## Upgrading

Use Check for Updates, or wait for the automatic check. **If automatic checks have stopped, the automatic check won't reach you: use File ▸ Check for Updates once.** After that, they work again.

## Changes

- **Updates (Windows and Linux):** automatic update checks could stop in time zones west of UTC, and run early east of it. The app stored the time of the last check in a form that moved by the zone's offset each time its settings were read and written. West of UTC the time could end up in the future, and no check was due until real time caught up. The time is now stored so it can't move. The first launch after updating runs one check, because the old time can't be trusted.
- **Updates (Windows and Linux):** after you chose Check for Updates soon after launch and then Install Update, a second, identical offer could appear over the download.
- **Redact Emails and Phone Numbers:** finds far more items in large or small-text captures on macOS, and matches international phone numbers and emails split by text recognition on all platforms.

The two update fixes don't apply to the macOS app, which updates through Sparkle.

## Known issues

- **Linux:** the About panel can open behind the main window. Bring it forward from the Activities overview.
- **Windows:** Email reports that the share is done as soon as you click it, not when Outlook's message window returns. A failure later in Outlook may go unreported.
- **Linux on Wayland:** the share sheet's Copy writes only the X11 clipboard. The toolbar's Copy button writes to both clipboards.
- **Windows 11:** pressing Esc in Open With's chooser doesn't bring the share sheet back (libs-sharing#26).
