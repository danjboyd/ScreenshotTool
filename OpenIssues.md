# Open Issues

- Toolbar icons still regress on GNUstep: the DateTracker-style toolbar baseline displays them, but GNUstep’s standard toolbar constrains rows to ~24 px so our 32 px art is clipped, and labels revert to the theme’s default colour if we re-enable the custom container.  
  **Investigations so far:** Swapped in the DateTracker toolbar logic under a feature flag, added a user preference that selects light vs. dark icon variants, and confirmed the PNGs load correctly. Attempted to rescale the bitmaps at runtime (mirroring `STRenderToolbarIcon`) but GNUstep discarded the resulting `NSImage` reps, so we reverted the change.  
  **Theory of the case:** GNUstep’s stock `NSToolbar` slots are hard-coded around 24 px and will continue to crop anything taller. Next steps are either (1) generate GNUstep-specific 24 px assets (possibly via ImageMagick) that include their own padding, or (2) revive `STToolbarItemContainer` for GNUstep only once we understand why the framework was replacing our view/label colours. Until then we’ll keep the DateTracker baseline for stability.
